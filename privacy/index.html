Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Wazabanga TV is an Android TV media player and source-management application. This Privacy Policy explains how Wazabanga TV handles information when you use the application.

User accounts

Wazabanga TV does not require users to create a Wazabanga account or sign in to use the application.

Your playlists and sources

Wazabanga TV allows users to add compatible playlists, streaming URLs and electronic programme guide sources. These sources are supplied and controlled by the user. When Wazabanga accesses a source selected by the user, network requests are made to the server hosting that source so that the requested content or programme information can be retrieved.

The operators of those external servers may receive technical information normally associated with an internet connection, such as the user's IP address. Wazabanga does not control the privacy practices of independently operated third-party sources.

Locally stored information

Information such as configured playlists, favourites, application preferences and programme-guide configuration may be stored locally on the user's Android TV device to provide application functionality.

Wazabanga does not use this locally stored information to build advertising profiles or sell it to third parties.

Content

Wazabanga does not provide, host, sell or distribute television channels, movies or copyrighted programming. Users are responsible for adding their own compatible sources and for ensuring they are authorized to access the content they use.

Advertising and tracking

The current Wazabanga TV application does not contain advertising functionality and does not use user information for targeted advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally by Wazabanga remains on the user's device until it is removed through application functionality, application data is cleared, or the application is uninstalled, as applicable.

Wazabanga does not maintain a Wazabanga user account requiring a separate account-deletion procedure.

Security

Wazabanga is designed to minimize collection of user information. Users should only add playlist, stream and programme-guide sources that they trust and are authorized to use.

Children

Wazabanga TV is a general-purpose media player and is not specifically directed at children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when Wazabanga TV's functionality or information-handling practices change. The effective date shown above identifies the current version.
