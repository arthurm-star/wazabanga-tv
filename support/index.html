WAZABANGA SUPPORT

Simple setup. Big-screen viewing.

Quick help for playlists, programme guides, Favorites and playback.

How do I add a playlist?

Open Playlists in Wazabanga TV, choose Add Playlist, enter a name and your compatible M3U or M3U8 URL, then save it.

How do I add programme-guide information?

Wazabanga supports XMLTV programme-guide sources. Configure your guide source in the app and Wazabanga will match available guide information to channels where possible.

How do Favorites work?

Highlight a channel and choose Add to Favorites. Saved channels appear in the Favorites section and remain available between sessions.

A channel does not play. What should I check?

Confirm the source URL is reachable and authorized, refresh the active playlist, and use Wazabanga diagnostics where available. Third-party source availability is controlled by its provider.

Does Wazabanga provide TV channels?

No. Wazabanga is a media player and source manager. Users add their own compatible, authorized playlists, streams and programme-guide sources.
