SUPPORT & FAQ

A player that keeps you in control.

Does Wazabanga provide TV channels?

No. Wazabanga is a media player and source manager. It does not host or provide television channels or copyrighted programming.

Does Wazabanga support programme guides?

Yes. Compatible XMLTV programme data can provide current programme, schedule, description and next-programme information.

What devices is this release for?

The current product is focused on Android TV and remote-first use.

Can I add my own playlist?

Yes. Only add sources and streams you are authorized to access.
